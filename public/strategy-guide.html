Key Formulas

Damage per hit

baseDmg × (1 + level × 0.03)

Then multiplied by attribute matchup (0.75× / 1.0× / 1.5×)

Attacks per second

baseSpd × (1 + level × 0.01)

DPS = damage × attack speed

Level-up cost

ceil(2 × currentLevel × stageMult)

Stage multipliers: IT×1 · R×1.5 · C×2 · U×2.5 · M×3 · Ultra×3.5

Max level

baseMax + (DP × dpBonus) + originBonus

Origin bonus = (currentStage − originStage) × 5. DP bonus per stage: IT×1, R×2, C×3, U×4, M×5, Ultra×5

Critical rules

  • Evolution resets level to 1. You must re-level from scratch after each digivolution.
  • Merge keeps the higher level. Fodder doesn't need leveling — merge level 1 sacrifices into your leveled main tower.
  • Origin caps evolution: In-Training origin → max Champion. Rookie origin → max Ultimate. Champion origin → max Mega.
  • AoE splash deals 50% damage to all enemies within 50px of the primary target. This is the #1 DPS multiplier in the game.
  • Boss lives penalty: Mid-phase bosses (waves 10, 30, 50, 70, 90) cost 2 lives. Phase bosses (waves 20, 40, 60, 80, 100) cost 5 lives. Never let a phase boss through!

Starter Pick

You pick 1 starter (placed for free). All other spawns draw from all 21 lines regardless of your pick. Your starter choice sets your early-game direction and one guaranteed free tower.

Best Pick: Dorimon (Data)

Dorimon → Dorumon → Dorugamon → DoruGremon → Alphamon

100% armor pierce at every stage. Alphamon is the highest Mega DPS (45.0 base) with pierce + AoE + execute. Also a DNA partner for Alphamon Ouryuken (Ultra).

Runner-up: Koromon (Vaccine)

Koromon → Agumon → Greymon → MetalGreymon → WarGreymon

Burn AoE at every stage — the best wave-clearing line in the game. WarGreymon + MetalGarurumon DNA fuses into Omegamon, the most versatile Ultra.

Phase 1: Waves 1–20

The Cheap Army

Income: 750 start → 5,070 cumulative  |  Enemies: In-Training & Rookie

Build Order

  1. Pre-wave 1: Place your free starter on a tile covering the upper serpentine (kill zone A). Spawn 2 random In-Training (150 DB) on adjacent tiles. Keep 600 DB for leveling.
  2. Waves 1–5: Level all 3 towers to level 5–7. Total cost ~120 DB. This gives ~12 DPS total — plenty for 72–258 HP waves.
  3. Waves 5–9: Spawn 2 more random In-Training (150 DB). Level to 5. You now have 5 towers, ~20 DPS.
  4. Wave 9 (pre-boss): Merge same-attribute pairs. You should get 1–2 merges for +1 DP each. The merge fodder is consumed, the survivor keeps the higher level.
  5. Wave 10 boss — Greymon (508 HP, slow): Your 3–4 surviving towers handle this easily. Boss walks slowly (91s path time).
  6. Waves 11–15: Keep spawning In-Training for merge fodder. Target: 2 towers with 3+ DP each. Cost per DP: 75 DB (one random spawn sacrificed).
  7. Waves 15–18: Evolve your 2 best DP-stacked towers to Rookie (80 DB each). At 3 DP, Rookie max level = 20 + 3×2 = 26.
  8. Wave 18–19: Level Rookies to 10–15. Evolve to Champion (120 DB). Champions at level 1 already outperform maxed In-Training.
  9. Wave 20 boss — Greymon Evolved (1,884 HP): 1–2 low-level Champions + Rookies handle this. Path time is 100+ seconds.

Boss Lives Penalty

Bosses that reach your base cost more than 1 life. Mid-phase bosses (waves 10, 30, 50, 70, 90) cost 2 lives. Phase bosses (waves 20, 40, 60, 80, 100) cost 5 lives. Letting a phase boss through is catastrophic — with 20 starting lives, that's 25% gone in one enemy. Prioritize boss kills above all else.

DP Farming Trick

Merge fodder does not need to be leveled. Spawn a level 1 In-Training (75 DB), immediately merge it into your main tower. Cost: 75 DB per DP. This is the cheapest DP gain in the game. Bank 3–5 DP before evolving.

Phase 2: Waves 21–40

The Champion Wall

Income: 5,070 → 14,340  |  Enemies: Champion tier (100–320 HP), Tanks 30–45% armor

Build Order

  1. Waves 21–25: Level your 2 Champions to 15–20. At level 20, Greymon does ~38 DPS with 25% AoE burn — this clears grouped Champion enemies. Total cost ~1,500 DB.
  2. Wave 25: Spawn 2 Rookie-origin towers (225 DB each, random). These will reach Ultimate and serve as your mid-game backbone. Pick Vaccine or Data for attribute coverage.
  3. Waves 25–30: Level Rookies to max (20), evolve to Champion (120 DB). You now have 4 Champions.
  4. Wave 30 boss — Devimon (4,675 HP, 25% armor, 2 lives): 4 Champions at level 15+ = ~120 DPS. Boss has 146s path time. Easy: 120 × 146 = 17,520 damage output vs 6,233 effective HP. But don't get careless — leaking a mid-phase boss costs 2 lives.
  5. Waves 31–35: KEY MOMENT — Spawn your first Champion-origin tower (750 DB random). This is your future Mega. Pick it carefully — Vaccine or Data attribute for the best Mega options (Alphamon, WarGreymon).
  6. Waves 35–39: Level everything. Sell your weakest In-Training towers (recover 50% investment). Push your best Champions to level 30+.
  7. Wave 40 boss — Myotismon (13,020 HP, 30% armor, Virus): FIRST REAL TEST. Stack Vaccine towers near the path. A Vaccine Champion at level 25 deals ~45 × 1.5 = 67 effective DPS against him. Need 3–4 focused Vaccine Champions.

Wave 40 — Myotismon (the skill check)

13,020 HP with 30% armor = 18,600 effective damage needed. He's Virus — Vaccine towers deal 1.5×. As a phase boss, Myotismon costs 5 lives if he reaches the base — that's 25% of your starting lives from one enemy. This is the wave where under-investing in Champions loses the game. You must have 3+ leveled Vaccine Champions.

Phase 3: Waves 41–60

The Ultimate Transition

Income: 14,340 → 28,910  |  Enemies: Ultimate (300–700 HP, 30–60% armor), Splitters

Build Order

  1. Waves 41–43: Evolve Rookie-origin Champions to Ultimate (160 DB each, resets to level 1). Level them to 10–15 immediately. Even a level 10 Ultimate outperforms a level 30 Champion.
  2. Waves 43–48: Level Ultimates to 25–30. Focus on Paildramon (Free, 28.8 base DPS) or DoruGremon (pierce AoE) if available.
  3. Wave 46 — SPLITTER WARNING: Mamemon wave. 10 enemies split into 20+ copies. Have AoE towers ready (Greymon, WarGrowlmon). Kill them before they split if possible.
  4. Wave 50 boss — SkullGreymon (12,350 HP, 30% armor, 2 lives): Ability: destroys ALL projectiles at 50% HP. Burst him down fast. 2 Ultimates + AoE Champions handle it.
  5. Waves 50–55: Spawn a second Champion-origin tower (750 DB). Start leveling your first Champion-origin through Ultimate. DP-merge Champions if you can afford it (750 DB per merge at Champion stage).
  6. Waves 55–58: Sell obsolete In-Training-origin towers. Their Champion cap can't keep up with Ultimate enemies. Recoup 50% and funnel into your Mega pipeline.
  7. Wave 60 boss — VenomMyotismon (22,160 HP, 35% armor, Virus, 5 lives): Ability: spawns 3 minions every 15s. As a phase boss, leaking him is devastating. Stack Vaccine Ultimates and bring armor break.

The Sell-and-Reinvest Loop

Your In-Training-origin towers cap at Champion. By wave 50, they're falling behind. Sell them (50% refund) and use the DigiBytes for Mega-path towers. A level 30 Champion you sell for ~350 DB can fund half a Champion-origin spawn. This loop is mandatory — hoarding old towers loses the game.

Specific Tower Targets

Must have by wave 50

  • Paildramon or DoruGremon at level 25+ (main DPS)
  • 1 armor break tower: Growlmon or Guilmon line
  • 1 slow/freeze tower: Garurumon line

Pipeline for later

  • 2 Champion-origin towers leveling through Champion (future Megas)
  • Ideally one is on the Alphamon or WarGreymon path
  • Start thinking about DNA partners

Phase 4: Waves 61–80

The Mega Race

Income: 28,910 → 48,580  |  Enemies: Mega (900–5,000 HP), 20–45% armor

Build Order

  1. Wave 61: Your Champion-origin tower should be at Ultimate, level 30–40 by now. Push to max Ultimate (level 55 with origin bonus) and evolve to Mega (200 DB, resets to level 1).
  2. Waves 61–65: Rush first Mega to level 20–25. Even a low-level Alphamon (75 base damage) outperforms a maxed Ultimate. Level-up cost: ~3,000 DB for level 1→25 at ×3 multiplier.
  3. Wave 66 — DANGER: Diaboromon splitters (×4). 7 enemies → 28 copies. If not killed before splitting, effective HP is ~170,000. Set all towers to "First" targeting and focus fire. AoE is your best friend here.
  4. Waves 66–69: Get second Mega online. Level first Mega to 35–40. Sell any remaining Rookie-origin Ultimates that are falling off.
  5. Wave 70 boss — Machinedramon (46,050 HP, 40% armor, 2 lives): Passive aura: reduces all tower ranges by 20%. Place towers close to path. 2 Megas at level 35 = ~350 DPS. With armor break active: 46,050 / 0.8 armor / 0.5 break = ~57,563 effective HP. 350 DPS × 100s path = 35,000 — this is tight. Level your Megas more or bring armor break.
  6. Waves 71–78: Push Megas to level 50–60. Spawn a third Champion-origin if budget allows (750 DB). This third Mega or DNA partner is your endgame insurance.
  7. Wave 80 boss — Omegamon (84,250 HP, 40% armor, 5 lives): Ability: 50% damage shield every 20s for 4s. A phase boss leak here costs 5 lives — potentially fatal. Need 3 Megas at level 40+ to brute force through the shield windows.

Wave 66 — The Diaboromon Trap

7 Diaboromon that each split into 4 copies on death. Total effective HP: ~170,000. If they reach the base, that's up to 28 lives lost. Counter: Focus-fire each Diaboromon before it dies near your base — kill them early on the path so the 4 split copies still have far to walk. AoE burn clears the splits.

Phase 5: Waves 81–100

The Endurance Check

Income: 48,580 → 74,050  |  Enemies: Ultra tier (3,000–5,000 HP × 3.5–4.0× scaling)

Build Order

  1. Wave 81–85: Push Megas to max level (70 base + origin bonus + DP bonus). Sell ALL remaining non-Mega towers. You need every DB for Ultra leveling.
  2. Wave 82–85: DNA Digivolve #1. Fuse WarGreymon + MetalGarurumon → Omegamon (requires both at max level, 3+ DP). Omegamon starts at level 1 but with 160 base DPS — even level 1 Ultra beats a level 50 Mega.
  3. Waves 85–89: Level Omegamon to 20–30. DPS at level 25: ~450. Each level costs ceil(2 × level × 3.5). Level 1→30 costs ~3,150 DB.
  4. Wave 90 boss — Omegamon Zwart (128,450 HP, 2 lives): Ability: stuns top 3 DPS towers for 3s every 15s. Spread your Megas so they're not all stunned at once. Your Ultra carries through the stun windows.
  5. Waves 90–94: DNA Digivolve #2 if possible (Alphamon + Ouryumon → Alphamon Ouryuken). Level all Ultras as high as budget allows. Every level matters.
  6. Wave 95 — THE REAL FINAL BOSS: 45 enemies totaling ~598,000 HP with 35–45% armor. Harder than wave 100. Your AoE splash + slow/freeze uptime is what wins here. If you survive this, you win the game.
  7. Wave 100 boss — Apocalymon (238,200 HP, 30% armor, 5 lives): Ability: stuns ALL towers for 2s at 25% HP. Single target with fewer effective HP than wave 95's horde — but leaking the final boss costs 5 lives, so don't get complacent. Your 2+ Ultras obliterate him. Victory.

Wave 95 > Wave 100

Wave 95 is the actual hardest wave. It has 2.5× the effective HP of the final boss. The Apocalymon fight has fewer raw HP — but leaking the final boss costs 5 lives, so don't write it off entirely. Still, if you survive wave 95 with 6+ lives, you've effectively won. Budget accordingly.

Golden Rule: Don't over-level towers you'll evolve soon

Evolution resets level to 1. If your Ultimate is 5 levels from max, evolve it to Mega rather than maxing it. Those last 5 levels are wasted DB. Only max a tower when it's at its final stage (or when you need the digivolve button to appear).

Overall Assessment

  • The game is beatable with optimal play, but there's almost no margin for error. Total budget (~74,050 DB) vs total investment needed (~73,500 DB) leaves only ~550 DB slack before sell-recycling.
  • Selling old towers is mandatory, not optional. The 50% recoup from selling 10+ obsolete In-Training/Rookie towers is what funds your endgame Megas.
  • DP farming is unintuitive but essential. A new player who doesn't merge early will hit level-cap walls around wave 50–60. The "spawn cheap fodder just to merge it" loop isn't obvious without this guide.
  • Wave 95 is harder than wave 100. The final boss Apocalymon (~238k HP single target) is easier than the wave 95 horde (~598k HP across 45 enemies). This rewards sustained board strength over single-target builds.
  • AoE + Slow/Freeze is the real meta. Raw DPS numbers are misleading. AoE effectively multiplies DPS by 2–4× on grouped enemies, and slow/freeze gives all towers 50–67% more shooting time. A team of AoE + CC towers with mediocre stats outperforms a team of high-DPS single-target towers.

Potential Balance Concerns

Wave 40 Myotismon

First serious boss. New players who spread across too many attribute types will lack focused Vaccine DPS. Consider adding a wave preview hint about attribute weakness.

Wave 66 Diaboromon

7 ×4 splitters = 28 enemies. Could feel unfair without warning. The wave preview shows "Splitter" tag but the ×4 multiplier (vs normal ×2) isn't obvious.

Champion-Origin Timing

Spawning at Champion costs 750 DB. Too early (wave 20) = economy crash. Too late (wave 55) = Megas come online too late for phase 4. The "right time" (wave 30–40) isn't signposted.

DP Farming Discovery

Optimal play requires merging level-1 fodder into your main tower. This non-obvious tactic is a 75 DB/DP bargain vs the alternative (leveling both towers to max first). Tutorial doesn't cover this.

What's Well-Balanced

Attribute Triangle

1.5× vs 0.75× is a meaningful 2× swing. Waves with mixed attributes reward diverse rosters. Boss attributes create clear "bring the right counter" moments.

AoE vs Single-Target

Both have clear roles. AoE clears waves, single-target kills bosses. The Diaboromon and horde waves (85–99) demand AoE, while boss-only waves demand focused damage.

DNA Digivolution Timing

Ultra power spike arrives exactly when you need it (wave 80+). The DP requirement gates ensure you've engaged with the merge system. Omegamon at 3 DP is accessible; Beelzemon BM at 6 DP rewards deep investment.

Difficulty Curve

Smooth early ramp (waves 1–30), clear skill checks (wave 40, 66, 80), and a demanding but fair endgame. Wave 95 as the true peak is good TD design — it tests your whole board, not just one tower.
